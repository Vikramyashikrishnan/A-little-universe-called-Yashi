A letter, just for you

A little space for all the words you want her to keep.

Yashiiii, happy birthday, my merii raniii. 🥹💗

I hope today gives you a hundred little reasons to smile, and that this new year of your life brings you closer to the dreams you carry so quietly in your heart. I hope you find beautiful things in ordinary days, people who appreciate the person you are, and moments that remind you how much life still has waiting for you.

If I could leave one wish beside you today, it would be that you always have room to grow into yourself without ever feeling you must be perfect to deserve kindness. Keep dreaming, keep laughing at the silly things, and keep that little spark that makes you unmistakably you.

I'm grateful that I get to know you, celebrate you, and make memories with you. This page is only a tiny keepsake, but I hope it makes you feel special today and gives you something to smile at again whenever you return to it.

Happy birthday, my Bulbul. May your world be gentle with you, and may this year be full of little wonders. 💗🌷

— someone who is very glad you were born 💌
